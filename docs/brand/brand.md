# Galene — brand reference (from docs/brand/galene-plataforma-de-marca.pdf, proposal dated 26 Nov 2026)

## Name & architecture
- Parent brand: **Galene** (Greek: "the calm sea") — "la casa del descanso femenino".
- **Diario de las 4am** — community + content, entry point. Signed "un diario de Galene". (The app's journal/content.)
- **Galene Atelier** — own brand made in Colombia. First product: silk eye mask (antifaz de seda).
- **La Selección** — multibrand store: the best sleep products in the world, tested before they get in.
- Decided: name Galene, multibrand store + own brand starting with textiles, Diario as community channel.
- In validation: US pronunciation, 35–50 entry segment, trademark availability.

## Promise / signature
- ES: **Noches en calma. Días enteros.**  US: **Calm nights. Whole days.**
- Alternatives: «Vuelve a la calma», «Para las que sostienen todo, también de noche».

## Audience & positioning
- Women ~30–50 who carry everything during the day and wake up at dawn.
- "Donde otros miden el sueño, Galene acompaña." Not pills, not devices that measure them.
- Vision: the reference for women's rest between Bogotá and New York.

## Pillars (brand values, not content categories)
Calma · Verdad (tested on me first, clear science, no miracle promises) · Oficio (beautiful, well-made, Colombian craft) · Compañía (no woman wakes up alone; the circle is part of the product).

## Voice
- Habla bajo y claro: short sentences, precise words, **no exclamation marks**.
- Afirma y explica: state the fact, then the reading behind it. Never corrects anyone.
- Acompaña, no diagnostica: share what was tested, cite evidence, leave medicine to doctors.
- Personality: the serene friend who already tried it. Elegant, honest, warm, precise. Never clinical, mystical, childish or anxious.
- YES: "Lo probé durante 21 noches. Te cuento qué cambió y qué no." / "No soy médica. Esto dice la evidencia y esto me funcionó a mí."
- NO: "El secreto para dormir 8 horas que nadie te contó." / "Cura tu insomnio en 7 días." / "Mujer guerrera, tú puedes con todo."

## Colors
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Marfil | #F6F1E9 | background, ~60% |
| Noche | #1F2340 | text and dark backgrounds, ~25% |
| Bruma | #C9CFD6 | support, ~5% |
| Marea | #5A6B7E | support, ~5% |
| Luna | #B8893E | accent only, ~5% |

Contrast rules (computed, WCAG):
- Noche on Marfil ~14:1 ✓ · Marea on Marfil ~4.9:1 ✓ (secondary text) · **Luna on Marfil ~2.8:1 ✗ — never gold text on light**; for gold labels on light use a darker gold (~#8A6528, verify ≥4.5:1).
- Marfil on Noche ~13.6:1 ✓ · Bruma on Noche ~9.8:1 ✓ (muted text, night) · Luna on Noche ~4.9:1 ✓ · **Marea on Noche ~2.8:1 ✗**.
- Gold buttons: **Noche text** on Luna (~4.9:1). Never Marfil on Luna.
Themes: **day** (Marfil-led) for web/landing; **night** (Noche bg, Marfil text, Bruma muted, Luna accent) default for the app.
**Never pink** as a brand color.

## Typography
- Headlines: **Cormorant Garamond** — sentence case, no italics except quotes. Use ≥24px, weight 500–600 (thin at small sizes).
- Text/UI: **Inter** — regular + medium. Small labels in spaced small caps.
- Logotype: small caps with wide letter spacing.

## Logo
Horizon symbol: lower line = calm sea, upper split line with a gold arc = the moon rising on the horizon at 4 AM. Light and dark versions; round avatar; horizontal signature; "Diario de las 4am — un diario de Galene" lockup.
Publicly tell the story as sea/horizon/moon (the Libra origin can read as astrology, and the brand is "never mystical").

## Photography
Low dawn light, silk and linen, real hands and gestures, women 30–50, no catalog poses. No perfect beds, no smiling sleeping women.
